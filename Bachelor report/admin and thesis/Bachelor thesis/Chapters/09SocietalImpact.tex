\section{Societal and Ethical Impact}


In communication with the customer on societal and ethical impact, key insights were given. Better visualization of \acrshort{ais} anomalies, is a tool that would transform how current work is done, rather than remove employment opportunities, leading to specialized workers, with access to more effective tools. As such the workplace impact of a fully developed software would not have a negative impacts on workers, neither would the use of better tools lead to loss of employment. As the system would be used to analyze data from large vessels, small vessels would not be affected by this. As a result no surveilance of individual persons would be committed if the tool was connected to real data. A fact of this is that boats are required to use \acrshort{ais} transponders by law \parencite{kystverket_ais_norge}, leading to the system not being a new form of surveillance on sea vessels.

Another point raised in communication with the customer is around the scenario if this could be used to stop illegal smuggling or immigration, but the vessel type being large, means the likelihood of this is low, as large vessels are less suited for illegal tasks like smuggling.

\section{Sustainability}

The sustainability impact of a software development project is generally lower than other fields. This is because of software not requiring the same amount of physical components. However, there are core sustainability factors such as maintainable code and choice of technology.\footnote{\url{https://beetroot.co/greentech/best-practices-of-sustainable-software-development/}}

\section{Environment}

The software not requiring physical components aside from computers, has a relatively low direct environmental footprint. Electricity being the only requirement to run the software, is the main environmental factor. Improved \acrshort{ais} detection could lead to action being taken sooner on faulty boats, leading to reduced environmental impact. The software itself is then unlikely to have a large environmental impact.
