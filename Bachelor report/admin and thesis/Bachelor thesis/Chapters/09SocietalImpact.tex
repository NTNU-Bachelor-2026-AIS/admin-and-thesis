\section{Societal and Ethical Impact}


In communication with the customer on societal and ethical impact, key insights were given. Better visualization of AIS anomalies, is a tool that would transform how current work is done, rather than remove employment opportunities, leading to specialized workers, with access to more effective tools. As such the workplace impact of a fully developed software would not have a negative impacts on workers, neither would the use of better tools lead to loss of employment. As the system would be used to analyze data from large vessels, small vessels would not be affected by this. As a result no surveilance of individual persons would be committed if the tool was connected to real data. A fact of this is that boats are required to use AIS transponders by law.
